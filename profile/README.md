# Manessa AI

**Real-time voice AI for deeper conversations.**

Most AI either sounds like customer support or like a roleplay app. [Manessa AI](https://manessaai.com) is built for the space in between: live, hands-free voice conversations with companions that keep up, remember over time, and push back when your reasoning is shaky.

### What makes it different
- **Real-time voice:** Talk out loud like a phone call, hands-free or push-to-talk, across 7 languages (EN, PL, ES, FR, IT, DE, RU).
- **Three distinct companions:** **Cipher** (curious and honest), **Emery** (warm and playful), and **Zenithar** (precise, deep on hard questions).
- **Long-term memory:** Daily recaps, week and month summaries, and facts that strengthen as they come up again — so conversations build instead of resetting.
- **Local-first & private by design:** Conversation history and memory live on your device, cross-device sync is optional and end-to-end encrypted with a password only you hold, and the AI models that write replies run with zero data retention. No ad tracking.
